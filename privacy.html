AutoShortsAI Postiz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Overview

AutoShortsAI Postiz is a personal project used to organize, prepare, and schedule short-form video content for social media accounts managed by the project owner.

2. Information and access

When the project connects to third-party platforms, it may access account information and content required to provide the requested publishing and scheduling features. The information accessed depends on the permissions granted by the user and the platform.

3. Use of information

Information accessed through platform integrations is used only to support the project's content management and publishing functions. It is not sold to third parties.

4. Storage and security

The project owner is responsible for protecting account credentials and configuration information. Access tokens and application secrets should be kept private and should not be included in public repositories.

5. Third-party platforms

TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Postiz, and other connected services process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data retention and deletion

The project owner may disconnect integrations and delete locally stored project data. Information held by third-party platforms remains subject to their respective policies and controls.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the project's features or integrations change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project owner through the GitHub repository where this document is published.
